What this first release does — and does not do
Included
Pair matching, incomplete-pair accounting, mean paired difference (B − A), standard deviation, paired effect size, deterministic bootstrap confidence interval, sign-flip permutation test, and downloadable records.
Not claimed
No causal discovery, no automatic study design approval, no correction for multiple comparisons, no missing-data imputation, and no guarantee that a study's assumptions are satisfied.


EvidenceBench is an independent analysis aid. Check the study design and assumptions before drawing conclusions. No account, tracking, analytics, or data upload is used by this page.
