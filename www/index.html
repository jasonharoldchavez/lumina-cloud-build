FEATURED STARTING POINT

Montgomery Bus Boycott · 1955–1956

The Montgomery Bus Boycott was a 13-month mass protest against racial segregation on public buses in Montgomery, Alabama. Rosa Parks' arrest on December 1, 1955, became a catalyst; the boycott involved many organizers, participants, and community networks.

This is a short orientation, not a complete account. The people involved held different roles and experiences; the sources below are starting points for further study.

How to read the labels

Documented history: claims supported by cited historical sources. Personal testimony: a first-person account. Family or community memory: an inherited or shared account. Interpretation: analysis or explanation. Disputed account: an account with material conflicts or uncertainty.

A label is not a truth score. A personal account can be important even when it cannot be independently corroborated. Drafts are not fact-checked, reviewed, or public.
